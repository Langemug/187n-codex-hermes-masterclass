The Founding Member Hoodie is a black heavyweight boxy hoodie made for people who don't walk with the crowd. It's the first piece of Drop 001 from Visionair Society, and it says what it means on the back.

A graphic back print that speaks for you

Across the back sits design 010: "THE MATRIX CAN'T HOLD ME." It's printed large, right under the hood, so it reads from a distance. On the front there's only a small VS logo on the left chest. Clean from the front, loud from behind.

Heavyweight fleece, 500 gsm

This is a heavy hoodie. The fleece weighs 500 gsm, which gives it a thick, structured feel that holds its shape instead of hanging loose and thin. It's built to be worn often, through colder months and long days out.

A boxy fit, on purpose

The cut is boxy: a wide body, wide shoulders and a shorter length than a regular hoodie. The result is a square, relaxed silhouette. If you want that look as designed, take the size closest to your usual hoodie. If you prefer a closer fit, go one size down.

Sizes and measurements

Available in S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Flat measurements in cm (length / shoulder / chest / sleeve): S 61 / 58.5 / 57 / 48 · M 63 / 60.5 / 59 / 49 · L 65 / 62.5 / 61 / 50 · XL 67 / 64.5 / 63 / 51 · 2XL 69 / 66.5 / 65 / 52.
