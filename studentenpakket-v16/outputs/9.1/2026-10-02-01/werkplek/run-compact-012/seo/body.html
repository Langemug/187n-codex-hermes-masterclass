Nº 012 is a statement back print hoodie for people who would rather play than watch. The back says it plainly: "BLUE PILLS ARE FOR SPECTATORS."

A statement back print

Design 012 is printed large across the back in white and purple, with a small two-tone capsule underneath. It reads from across the room and needs no explanation for the people who get it. The message is simple: you choose to take part, or you choose to look on from the side.

Heavyweight fleece, 500 gsm

The hoodie is made of heavyweight fleece at 500 gsm. That weight gives it a thick, structured feel that holds its shape instead of hanging thin. It's made to be worn often, from cold mornings to late nights.

Boxy fit

The cut is boxy: wide through the body, wide shoulders and shorter than a regular hoodie. That creates a square, relaxed silhouette. For the look as designed, take the size closest to your usual hoodie. For a closer fit, go one size down.

Sizes and measurements

Available in S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Flat measurements in cm (length / shoulder / chest / sleeve): S 61 / 58.5 / 57 / 48 · M 63 / 60.5 / 59 / 49 · L 65 / 62.5 / 61 / 50 · XL 67 / 64.5 / 63 / 51 · 2XL 69 / 66.5 / 65 / 52. A difference of 1–3 cm may occur.
