The pot and refill starter kit is the first step in the Oefenmerk routine. You receive one pot and one refill together. After that, every next delivery contains a refill only, so you keep using the same pot.

What is in the starter kit

The starter kit holds two items: the pot and the first refill. The pot is meant to stay with you. The refill goes inside it. That is the whole idea: buy the container once, and replace only what you use.

What comes after the starter

From the second delivery on, you only receive a refill. There is no new pot in later deliveries. This keeps every following order smaller and simpler than the first one.

Test pricing

The prices on this page are test assumptions used for calculations, not a live offer. The starter is set at €49. Each following refill delivery is set at €29 per month. These amounts can change before anything is sold.

What this page does not cover

This page says nothing about what is inside the refill or what it does. That information is not available in the test data, so we leave it out instead of guessing.

Is the starter kit right for you?

Choose the starter kit if you do not have the pot yet. If you already have the pot from an earlier order, you only need a refill delivery. If you are not sure which delivery you will get next, check your last order: a starter means your next delivery is a refill.
