A wave graphic back print

The back carries a simple wave graphic, printed large so it reads from a distance. The front stays quiet, with only a small logo on the left chest. Keep the print size and position the same on every size, so the wave graphic hoodie looks the way it does in the photos.

Heavyweight fleece

The hoodie is made of heavyweight fleece. Write the exact weight here only if your supplier confirms it in writing. A thick fleece keeps its shape after washing and feels structured instead of thin, which is often the first thing buyers notice when they unpack a heavyweight hoodie.

Boxy fit

The cut is boxy: wide through the body and slightly shorter than a regular hoodie. For the look as designed, take your usual size. For a closer fit, go one size down. Between two sizes? Compare the chest width with a hoodie you already own and like.
