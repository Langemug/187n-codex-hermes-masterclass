Nº 009 is a glitch graphic hoodie for people who stopped trying to run like everyone else. The message on the back is short: "CERTIFIED GLITCH." The error is the point.

A glitch graphic back print

The back carries design 009: the words "CERTIFIED GLITCH." in white and purple, framed like an official stamp, with "VISIONAIR SOCIETY · ISSUED" underneath. It's printed large across the back so it reads from a distance. The front stays quiet, with only a small VS logo on the left chest.

Heavyweight fleece, 500 gsm

The hoodie is made of heavyweight fleece at 500 gsm. That weight gives it a thick, structured feel that keeps its shape instead of hanging thin. It's made to be worn a lot, from cold mornings to late nights out.

Boxy fit

The cut is boxy: wide through the body, wide shoulders and shorter than a regular hoodie. That gives a square, relaxed silhouette. For the look as designed, take the size closest to your usual hoodie. For a closer fit, go one size down.

Sizes and measurements

Available in S, M, L, XL and 2XL. Flat measurements in cm (length / shoulder / chest / sleeve): S 61 / 58.5 / 57 / 48 · M 63 / 60.5 / 59 / 49 · L 65 / 62.5 / 61 / 50 · XL 67 / 64.5 / 63 / 51 · 2XL 69 / 66.5 / 65 / 52. A difference of 1–3 cm may occur.

Visionair Society

Every Visionair Society design has a number. Nº 009 is for the ones who don't fit the system and don't plan to.
