05Voice

Rules

  • English for all brand and store copy. Talk to "you".
  • Short. 2–7 words per sentence. Full stop, no exclamation marks.
  • Statement first. Explain only what someone needs to buy.
  • Work in pairs: think / act, talk / build, apart / together.
  • Hard, not mean. Never name or mock competitors.
  • Always mention both the design and the number.

Never

  • Fake scarcity ("only 3 left").
  • Reviews, testimonials or member counts that don't exist.
  • Material claims beyond "heavyweight cotton-blend fleece".
  • "No refunds" or anything against consumer law.
  • Soft marketing: "discover", "our premium collection".
  • Promises of success.

07Art direction

There are no real product photos yet. Use these rules for the first shoot and for briefing AI images.

Product photos

  • Black hoodie on black or dark concrete. Hard side light, deep shadows.
  • Always one shot of the back design and one close-up of the number.
  • Show the real garment only. No AI image presented as the product.
  • Purple only as light accent or logo, never a purple backdrop.

UGC

  • Real builders in their real place: studio, desk, street, night shift.
  • Phone-shot is fine. Natural light or street light, no ring light glow.
  • They say what they build, not how good the hoodie is.
  • Written permission from every person shown.

Ads

  • Hook in the first second, as caption and on screen.
  • Hoodie or person in frame by second five.
  • One hook per ad; same body and button across tests.
  • End card: logo, "Join the society", nothing else.

Mood (reference only)

  • City at dusk, parking decks, concrete, sodium lights.
  • The AI campaign concept "Which one would you wear?" sets the mood. It is not a product image.
